About Me

My journey, my story, my passion

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Uttara University with a passion for solving problems — whether through code, data, or direct human interaction.

My journey started with teaching (7 years, secondary-level Computer Science), then moved into field operations as a Technician at Duranto Internet Service Provider for 4 years — where I learned how to handle real customer challenges, troubleshoot on the spot, and stay calm under pressure.

Currently, I work as a Data Entry Operator at Matrix Sweater Ltd., where I manage production data, inventory logs, and workforce records. This combination of technical, customer-facing, and data-driven experience has shaped me into a well-rounded professional.

I'm also exploring AI-assisted development — building websites using Google AI Studio and prompt engineering to stay future-ready.
